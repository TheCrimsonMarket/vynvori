# Consumer pricing context

Official pages checked 12 October 2026. These are subscriptions/catalog offers, not API prices or customer acquisition estimates.

| Product | Public observation | Comparability |
|---|---|---|
| Krea Pro | $35 monthly / displayed $21 per month on annual billing; 20,000 units | Includes broader tools; model-specific consumption differs |
| OpenArt Starter | $14/seat/month regular price, 4,000 credits; other tiers have first-month promos | “Up to” image counts are not a fixed price for premium renders |
| Ideogram Plus | $20 monthly / $15 annual equivalent, 2,400 credits | Model/render setting determines credits; API is billed separately |
| Midjourney Basic/Standard/Pro/Mega | $10/$30/$60/$120 monthly | GPU-time/subscription economics; manual reference only |
| Printful oversized boxy tee | Displayed catalog $10.24; drop-shoulder tee $20.71 | Physical catalog offers; shipping/taxes/placement/region differ |

Sources: [Krea](https://www.krea.ai/pricing), [OpenArt](https://openart.ai/pricing), [Ideogram](https://ideogram.ai/pricing/), [Midjourney](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/27870484040333-Comparing-Midjourney-Plans), [Printful](https://www.printful.com/custom/mens/t-shirts).

Do not compare a Vynvori guided session (3 concepts + 2 edits + a final) directly to a subscription's advertised maximum image count. Separate the paid design experience from garment retail economics. A standalone AI-shirt-designer price was not verified with the same scope; mark N/A rather than treat Printful's physical catalog as an equivalent AI session. No subscription, garment or inventory was purchased.
