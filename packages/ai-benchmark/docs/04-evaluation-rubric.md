# Independent blind review

Give each reviewer the review folder and this rubric. Hide the raw ledger, costs, model names and mapping until both raters submit. Review each sample 1 (unusable), 2 (major defects), 3 (usable with correction), 4 (strong), 5 (excellent).

| Metric | Weight in complete benchmark | Observe |
|---|---:|---|
| Artistic originality | 25% | Coherent original motif, wearability, distinctive composition |
| Moodboard fidelity | 20% | Palette, intended symbolism and synthesis without copying |
| Edit/identity preservation | 20% | Requested delta and absence of unintended changes |
| Print suitability | 20% | Contrast, negative space, edge/alpha quality and native pixels |
| Cost per accepted output | 10% | Computed after blind acceptance, never shown to raters |
| Latency/reliability | 5% | Computed from all run records after blind scoring |

Concepts/finals do not establish localized edit retention; the CLI excludes that 20% and renormalizes the observed quality dimensions. Full 100% weighted business ranking is NOT implemented/claimed in the screen. The leaderboard reports quality, accepted costs and latency separately, allowing founders to choose weights once print and territory gates are resolved.

Acceptance requires completed delivery, two distinct reviewers, both accepting with an IP pass, and disagreement <=1 quality point. `accepted` is artistic/digital acceptance, distinct from production qualification. Full-zone native PPI and transparent-background checks form separate print gates. Neither a high score nor a cheap image can override a failed production gate.

For edits, compare the common source beside the result. Color edit: purple should change to #e3aa50 while shape and other colors stay intact. Local edit: only the top-right star should appear. Describe any identity, silhouette, placement, lighting or palette drift. Do not infer identity retention from a beautiful new character.

Review full-resolution alpha and edges before checking inspection fields. Native opaque images and fake checkerboard backgrounds are not transparent. Physical DTF assessment, pressing/washing and supplier confirmation remain separate. No agent-invented scores appear in this report.
