# Credit proposal — pending acceptance evidence

No denomination or retail price is activated. Minimum proposed top-up remains $5 as specified in the handoff. Final denomination is N/A until accepted-design costs, payment fees and production cleanup are known. The simulator exposes the 3× / 3.5× / 4× multipliers with unresolved values explicitly blank.

Evaluate $0.01 and $0.05 candidate credit units after measurement; these are usability alternatives, not selected pricing. For denomination d, an event priced at target P requires `ceil(P/d)` credits and costs that count × d USD. A $5 pack contains 5/d credits; bundles must show exact required top-ups and remainder. Test that rounding preserves the intended post-fee discount floor.

Operation table to populate: promotional first preview, subsequent preview/concept, edit, standard final, premium final, optional digital download. Guided bundle = 3 concepts + 2 edits + standard final; premium upgrade should charge only its incremental qualified cost rather than silently rebilling the standard final.

Treat the single free first-preview expense as acquisition spend. At conversion rate q, acquired-customer trial allocation is free-preview effective cost / q; test q = 5%, 10%, 20% and 40% with explicit assumptions. This is a sensitivity formula, not a measured conversion rate.

Shirt redemption stays N/A until landed garment contribution is audited. Store an entitlement with design ID, eligible net design spend, expiry, cap and consumption state; physical redemption must not double-spend wallet credits. Model rate × eligible spend against actual margin rather than choosing a percentage now.

Proposed behavior for founder review: refund reserved credits for provider non-delivery; retain an unresolved reservation until reconciliation; charge only one successful idempotent job; cancellation before submission releases reservation; cancellation after submission waits for billing resolution. Clarify unused balances, dispute/chargeback handling, download rights, gallery display vs resale permissions and cross-border tax treatment before checkout work. These are proposals, not legal determinations or live wallet code.
